How RF works

Proof Before Authority.

Intelligence proposes the operation. Independently checked recovery evidence determines whether bounded authority can exist.

01Propose

Name the change.

Submit a typed operation, its target and required recovery through RF's supported contract.

02Rehearse

Establish recovery evidence.

Exercise the supported change and recovery procedure on a disposable shadow within the declared scope.

03Verify

Bind the authority.

Independently verify evidence and bind authority to the operation, resources, state and expiry.

04Enforce

Execute or refuse.

The native boundary checks the applicable conditions. Invalid evidence, drift or consumed authority causes refusal.

Which operations can Pincr govern?+

The current runnable pilot supports narrow operation classes across PostgreSQL, Kubernetes and Linux. The initial examples are adding a nullable column, setting Deployment replicas and replacing a designated file. Coverage and native qualification differ by domain. Each evaluation defines its exact supported scope; this is not arbitrary SQL, shell or cloud-command execution.

Does RF replace identity, permissions or approval?+

RF adds a recovery requirement to the execution decision. Identity, organizational policy and other approvals still apply. Demonstrated recoverability does not establish that a change is desirable or that every consequence is covered. Alternative execution routes must also be controlled for the governed boundary to be effective.

Where does RF run?+

RF enforcement operates through its native infrastructure integrations. The intended enterprise model combines customer-deployed enforcement with a web inspection experience. Deployment, credentials, evidence handling and operating responsibilities are agreed for each evaluation. A generally available hosted enterprise console is not being offered here.

Can Fuse authorize or execute an operation?+

No. Fuse is an RF-specialist intelligence capability in development. Its customer-facing role is to help explain permitted records and evidence. It does not qualify its own recommendations or grant execution authority. RF functions independently of Fuse.

What is available today?+

Pincr is developing the platform and discussing scoped enterprise evaluations. Technical evidence comes from bounded internal qualification; no external production acceptance or formal design partners are established. Start with a conversation about your workflow and an agreed staging scope.

Demonstrated recoverability does not establish universal reversibility, broad production certification or the desirability of a change. Effects outside a declared recovery boundary remain outside that claim.
