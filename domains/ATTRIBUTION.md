# Domain mark attribution

These marks identify the infrastructure domain described on the Pincr platform page. They are not Pincr marks and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

- **PostgreSQL Slonik:** `postgresql.png`, from the PostgreSQL project press kit: <https://www.postgresql.org/media/img/about/press/elephant.png>. The PostgreSQL wiki says logo use is subject to its trademark policy: <https://wiki.postgresql.org/wiki/Logo> and <https://www.postgresql.org/about/policies/trademarks/>.
- **Kubernetes:** `kubernetes.svg`, from the Kubernetes project repository: <https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/logo/logo.svg>. See the project's branding guidance and the Linux Foundation trademark guidance: <https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/logo/usage_guidelines.md>.
- **Linux Tux:** `linux-tux.png`, the original Tux image by Larry Ewing, retrieved from Wikimedia Commons: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tux.png>. The Linux kernel FAQ credits Larry Ewing and states that Tux is free to use, including commercially, with proper credit: <https://www.kernel.org/faq.html>.

The logos are kept in their original colors and proportions and rendered as small domain identifiers. No logo is recolored, combined with the Pincr mark, or used to suggest endorsement.
